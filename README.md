# Bee Health Early Warning System 

Honeybees are vital to crop pollination and therefore to the security of our food system. However, beekepers have recently faced an alarming rate of colony collapse. For example, according to the Apiray Inspectos of America, in 2023-24 and 2024-25 beekeepers lost approximately [55% of their managed colonies. ](https://apiaryinspectors.org/US-beekeeping-survey-23-24?) The causes of colony collapse are complex and not entirely well understood.

Motivated to build an early warning system that could warn beekepers of a likely collapse , this project was taken on in two separate phases with different dataset.
