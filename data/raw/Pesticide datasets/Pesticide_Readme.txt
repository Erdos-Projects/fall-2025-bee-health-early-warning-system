This README provides a description of the two datasets, LowEstimate_AgPestUsebyCropGroup92to19.txt and HighEstimate_AgPestUsebyCropGroup92to19.txt.

Datasets
LowEstimate_AgPestUsebyCropGroup92to19.txt: This dataset contains the lower-bound estimates of agricultural pesticide use by crop group from 1992 to 2019.

HighEstimate_AgPestUsebyCropGroup92to19.txt: This dataset contains the upper-bound estimates of agricultural pesticide use by crop group from 1992 to 2019.

Variables
Both datasets share the same structure, with the following variables:

State_FIPS_code: The Federal Information Processing Standard (FIPS) code for the state. 

State: The name of the state. 

Compound: The name of the pesticide compound. 

Year: The year the data was recorded. 

Units: The units of measurement for pesticide use, which is in kilograms (kg). 

Corn: The estimated amount of pesticide used on corn crops. 

Soybeans: The estimated amount of pesticide used on soybean crops. 

Wheat: The estimated amount of pesticide used on wheat crops. 

Cotton: The estimated amount of pesticide used on cotton crops. 

Vegetables_and_fruit: The estimated amount of pesticide used on vegetable and fruit crops. 

Rice: The estimated amount of pesticide used on rice crops. 

Orchards_and_grapes: The estimated amount of pesticide used on orchard and grape crops. 

Alfalfa: The estimated amount of pesticide used on alfalfa crops. 

Pasture_and_hay: The estimated amount of pesticide used on pasture and hay. 

Other_crops: The estimated amount of pesticide used on other crops.
